Does Whisper hear nothing?

Play a sound with no speech in it and Whisper may still write “Thank you for watching!”. Here every clip goes through five methods at once: plain Whisper, two projection settings, our affine variant, and plain Whisper with a lower no-speech threshold.

Nongmaithem Hans & Ravikant Sharma · Instructors: Dr. Anusha Prakash and Dr. Arun Kumar Sharma · Read the report

How to read this

The gauge

Whisper's first decoding step gives a no-speech probability, pns. The shaded part of each gauge is the reject zone above the threshold τ: a dot that lands there means the output is thrown away.

pns of plain Whisper after the edit gate γ

The projection

If pns is at least the gate γ, the clip is decoded again with a low-rank “hallucination subspace” removed from one decoder layer (Eq. 1), or moved to the mean of silent clips (affine, Eq. 2). That usually pushes pns to the right, into the reject zone.

The verdicts

✓ Correct speech transcribed, or no output for a non-speech sound. ✗ Hallucinated text for a non-speech sound. ! Falsely rejected real speech thrown away. For speech, the output is compared with the LibriSpeech reference: wrong words, missing words and extra words.
